== Biography ==

'''Hester Welch''' was born in 1768 in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina, to [[Welch-185|Major Nicholas Welch]] (c. 1738–1814) and [[Moore-51757|Elizabeth E. Moore]] (c. 1740–c. 1780-1782). She was one of at least sixteen children born to this prominent Loyalist family during the Revolutionary War era.

=== Family Context and Naming Honor ===

Hester's name carries significant family meaning. She was almost certainly named in honor of her maternal grandmother, '''[[Winston-1414|Hester Winston]]''' (also documented as "Hester Moore" after her marriage), who married '''Captain Moses Moore''' (1718–1797) of Indian Creek, Lincoln County, North Carolina.

The Moore and Welch families were neighbors and close associates in the North Carolina backcountry:
* '''Captain Moses Moore''' and his wife Hester Winston lived on Indian Creek
* '''Major Nicholas Welch''' lived "just above Moore on Indian Creek"
* A '''1774 deed in Tryon County, North Carolina''' explicitly names "Moses Moore and his wife Hester"
* Both families were heavily involved in Loyalist activities during the American Revolution

=== The "Two Hesters" Question ===

Genealogical records document '''two daughters named Hester''' attributed to Nicholas Welch and Elizabeth Moore:

'''Hester Welch''' (this profile):
* Born: 1768
* Location: Mecklenburg County, North Carolina
* Listed as first daughter named Hester

'''[[Welsh-64|Hester Welsh]]''' (second daughter):
* Born: c. 1773
* Location: Tryon County, North Carolina
* Listed as second daughter named Hester

The presence of two daughters with the same name was common in colonial families and typically indicates:
1. The first Hester died young, and the name was reused to preserve the family honor
2. Record-keeping errors conflating two separate records for the same person
3. Different spelling variants (Welch vs. Welsh) representing the same individual

Further research is needed to clarify whether these represent one person with variant birth dates/locations, or two separate daughters.

=== Revolutionary War Period and Family Exile ===

Hester's early childhood coincided with the tumultuous Revolutionary War years. Her family faced severe consequences for their Loyalist allegiance:

* '''June 1780:''' Her father fought at the Battle of Ramsour's Mill
* '''October 1780:''' Patriot victory at King's Mountain sealed the Loyalist defeat
* '''1780-1782:''' The Welch family was banished from North Carolina; Hester's mother Elizabeth died during this forced exile
* '''1782:''' Her father arrived in St. Johns Town, East Florida as "a widower with six children"
* '''1784:''' The family relocated to New Providence, Bahamas

As Hester was approximately 12-14 years old during the exile period, she would have been among the children who accompanied her widowed father through these migrations, though specific details of her whereabouts during this period are not documented.

=== Later Life ===

Details of Hester's adult life, marriage, and descendants remain undocumented in available sources. Unlike several of her siblings whose lives are well-documented through land records, military service (War of 1812), and probate files, Hester's later years have not been traced in historical records.

=== Other "Hesters" in the Connected Families ===

The name Hester was prominent in the interconnected Moore and Welch families:

'''[[Winston-1414|Hester Winston Moore]]''' (c. 1720s–unknown):
* Wife of Captain Moses Moore
* Mother of Elizabeth E. Moore (Hester's mother)
* Documented in 1774 Tryon County deed
* Grandmother for whom Hester Welch was likely named

'''Hester Moore Roberts''' (1762–1838):
* Daughter of Moses Moore and Hester Winston
* Aunt of Elizabeth E. Moore (if Moses Moore was Elizabeth's father)
* Married Joshua Roberts, a Patriot soldier
* Lived on Moses Moore's old homeplace on Indian Creek after the Revolutionary War

The strong presence of the name "Hester" in both the Moore and Welch families reflects the close kinship ties and naming traditions of these North Carolina frontier families.

== Research Notes ==

'''Priority Research Questions:'''
1. Are Hester Welch (b. 1768) and [[Welsh-64|Hester Welsh]] (b. c. 1773) the same person or two separate daughters?
2. Did the first Hester die in childhood, leading to the naming of a second daughter?
3. What was Hester's fate during the family's exile to Georgia, East Florida, and the Bahamas (1780-1784)?
4. Did Hester marry? If so, to whom, and where did she settle?
5. Are there descendants who could provide DNA evidence to confirm family relationships?

'''Research Strategies:'''
* Search Bahamas vital records (1784-1800) for marriage or death of Hester Welch
* Examine South Carolina and Kentucky records (1800-1810) where Nicholas Welch appeared with family
* Search Giles County, Tennessee records (1810-1820) where Nicholas Welch settled
* Check for land grants or deed records in Nicholas Welch's name that might mention daughter Hester
* Review War of 1812 pension applications for any mentions of sisters of documented veterans

'''Connection to Moses Moore Lineage:'''

If '''Captain Moses Moore''' was Elizabeth E. Moore's father (see [[Moore-51757|Elizabeth E. Moore]] Research Notes for conflicting parentage theories):
* Hester Winston would be Elizabeth's mother (Hester Welch's maternal grandmother)
* Hester Moore Roberts (1762-1838) would be Elizabeth's sister (Hester Welch's maternal aunt)

DNA testing of descendants could help confirm or refute this family connection.

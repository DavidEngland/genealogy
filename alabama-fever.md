# Alabama Fever
## A Song of the Kentucky-to-Alabama Migration (1816-1820)

**For Mary Kutter**

---

### Verse 1
There was a time back in old Kentucky  
When the tobacco fields done turned to dust  
Folks heard tell of Alabama cotton  
Rich black soil, if you had the guts  

They called it "Alabama Fever"  
Spread like wildfire through the hills  
From Lincoln County down to Pulaski  
Everybody caught the cotton chills  

### Chorus
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
Burning in our bones  
Down to the Shoals where the river sings  
Gonna make ourselves a home  
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
Can't be satisfied  
'Til we reach that Tennessee River  
Where the waters run wild  

### Verse 2
The Injuns got the land, we know it  
Cherokee, Chickasaw, Creek held fast
But we can rent it for a little while  
Just a matter of time—won't last  

Government's making treaties every season  
Pushing borders further west  
We'll be waiting at the new-drawn line  
First to claim, first to rest  

### Chorus
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
Burning in our bones  
Down to the Shoals where the river sings  
Gonna make ourselves a home  
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
Can't be satisfied  
'Til we reach that Tennessee River  
Where the waters run wild  

### Verse 3
From Kentucky through Tennessee borderlands  
Wagons rolling day and night  
Through the Cumberland, through the valleys  
Chasing cotton-farming dreams so bright  

Muscle Shoals, they call it Muscle Shoals  
Where the river roars and bends  
White water singing ancient stories  
Of beginnings and of ends  

### Bridge
The river sings, oh the river sings  
Of all who came before  
The river sings, oh the river sings  
But we don't hear no more  

We got Alabama Fever  
Nothing else will do  
Fresh land, cotton prices rising  
We're starting something new  

### Verse 4
Valentine Hargrove left his Kentucky home  
Joseph Lewis and the Whitley clan  
Crossed the Tennessee state line together  
Every woman, child, and man  

Left behind the worn-out pastures  
Left behind the rocky ground  
Headed for the Alabama Territory  
Where the cotton grows year-round  

### Final Chorus
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
Burning in our bones  
Down to the Shoals where the river sings  
We're gonna make our home  
Alabama Fever, Alabama Fever  
We done paid the cost  
But the river keeps on singing low  
'Bout everything we lost  

### Outro
And the Tennessee River keeps on singing  
Through the Muscle Shoals so strong  
Singing 'bout the ones who had it first  
Singing 'bout the ones who're gone  
Yeah, the river that remembers  
Keeps on singing its old song...

---

## Musical Notes

**Suggested style:** Appalachian folk, mountain ballad  
**Tempo:** Moderate, driving rhythm (like a wagon rolling)  
**Key:** D minor or A minor (for a somewhat melancholy, historical feel)  
**Instrumentation:** Fiddle, banjo, acoustic guitar, upright bass, possibly dulcimer

The "river that sings" refers to the **Tennessee River at Muscle Shoals**, where water rushing over rocky shoals creates a distinctive sound that Native peoples and early settlers described as singing or roaring.

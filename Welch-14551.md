== Biography ==

'''Nicholas Welch''' was born in 1786 in Anderson, Pendleton County, South Carolina, to '''[[Welch-11475|Nicholas William Welch Jr.]]''' (1762–1830) and '''Margaret "Peggy" Hembree''' (1764–unknown). His father was 24 years old and his mother 22 at the time of his birth. Nicholas lived his entire life in the South Carolina upcountry, dying on 3 December 1870 in South Carolina at the age of 84.

=== Family Background ===
Nicholas was the grandson of '''[[Welch-185|Major Nicholas Welch]]''' (c. 1738–1814), a prominent Loyalist officer who served in the Royal British Army during the American Revolution. His grandfather fought at the Battle of Ramsour's Mill and was eventually exiled from North Carolina, settling first in East Florida and the Bahamas before his family returned to the United States.

His father, '''Nicholas William Welch Jr.''' (1762–1830), was born during the Revolutionary War period and was part of the generation that reconciled with the new American republic. The family migrated from North Carolina through South Carolina and Georgia, with Nicholas William Jr. eventually settling in the South Carolina upcountry.

=== Marriage and Family ===

Nicholas married '''Mrs. Nicholas Welch''' (name unknown). They had at least one daughter documented in genealogical records, though further details about his wife and children remain to be researched.

=== Life in South Carolina ===

Nicholas's life was centered in the Pendleton District of South Carolina:

* '''1786:''' Born in Anderson, Pendleton County, South Carolina
* '''1790:''' Family living in Laurens County, South Carolina (Nicholas age 4)
* '''c. 1790–1800:''' Resided in Pendleton County, South Carolina for approximately 10 years
* '''1870:''' Died in South Carolina at age 84

The Pendleton District (established 1789) was a large administrative region in northwestern South Carolina that was later divided into several counties including Anderson, Pickens, and Oconee. Nicholas's residency in this area places him among the early settlers of the South Carolina upcountry.

=== Later Life and Death ===

Nicholas Welch died on '''3 December 1870''' in South Carolina at the age of 84. He lived through remarkable periods of American history, including:
* The early Republic and westward expansion
* The War of 1812
* The antebellum period
* The Civil War and early Reconstruction

His longevity (84 years) and consistent residence in South Carolina contrasts with many of his Welch relatives who migrated westward to Kentucky, Tennessee, and Alabama during the early 19th century.

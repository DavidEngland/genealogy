== Biography ==

Raymond A Lawson was born on 4 March 1920 in Florence, Lauderdale, Alabama, United States. His father, George Henry Clay Lawson, was 44 years old at the time of his birth, and his mother, Eliza Ann Pigg, was 39.

Raymond married Carolyn Grace McFall on 28 November 1947 in Russellville, Franklin, Alabama, United States.

In 1950, Raymond lived in Election Precinct 10 Florence, Lauderdale, Alabama, United States.

Raymond died on 5 January 2005 in Lauderdale, Alabama, United States, at the age of 84. He was buried in Florence, Lauderdale, Alabama, United States.

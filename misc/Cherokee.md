## **Cherokee Syllabary Additions:**
- **Wu-te-he**: ᏭᏖᎯ (used throughout)
- **Sequoyah**: ᏍᏏᏉᏯ (Ssiquaya)
- **Clan names with syllabary**:
  - Paint Clan: Ani-Wodi / ᎠᏂᏬᏗ
  - Wolf Clan: Ani-Wahya / ᎠᏂᏩᏯ
  - Deer Clan: Ani-Kawi / ᎠᏂᎧᏫ
  - Bird Clan: Ani-Tsisqua / ᎠᏂᏥᏍᏆ
  - Holly Clan: Ani-Gilahi / ᎠᏂᎩᎳᎯ
  - Long Hair Clan: Ani-Gilohi / ᎠᏂᎩᎶᎯ
  - Wild Potato Clan: Ani-Gatagehi / ᎠᏂᎦᏔᎨᎯ
- **Historical figures**: Moytoy (ᎼᏱᏙᎢ), Oconostota (ᎣᎪᎾᏍᏔᏘ), Doublehead (ᏔᎵᏧᏍᎦ), Old Tassel (ᎪᏁᏔᏎᎵ)
- **Places**: Echota (ᎠᎹᏯ ᎢᏣ), Trail of Tears (ᏄᎾᎯ ᏚᎾᏙᎶᎯᎵᎩ)
- **Common words**: Wado (ᏩᏙ / Thank you), Osiyo (ᎣᏏᏲ / Hello), Tla-yv-sv (ᏝᏴᏒ / Goodbye), A-ni-tsi-s-qua (ᎠᏂᏥᏍᏆ / Bird), A-ni-wa-hya (ᎠᏂᏩᏯ / Wolf), A-ni-ka-wi (ᎠᏂᎧᏫ / Deer)

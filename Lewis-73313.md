== Biography ==

Joseph Lewis of Goochland was a prominent figure in the 18th-century Virginia "Western Aristocracy," serving as a central administrative and religious pillar of Henrico and Goochland counties for over two decades.

=== Civic and Religious Leadership ===
The Vestry Book of Henrico Parish documents Joseph as a "Gentleman Vestryman" attending numerous meetings between 1752 and his final documented meeting on December 17, 1773. He was repeatedly elected to the prestigious office of '''Church Warden''', a position that involved managing the parish's financial obligations, including the care of the poor and the maintenance of church buildings like the "Upper Church" in Richmond. He held this office during terms in 1753, 1754, 1755, 1757, 1758, and 1764. 

In 1766, he was appointed '''Sheriff of Henrico County''' and also served as the '''Parish Collector''', a role requiring him to provide a bond for the faithful collection of the parish tobacco levy from the 1,918 tithable residents to pay the rector's salary and parish debts.

=== Family Standing ===
Joseph Lewis utilized his social standing to forge alliances that connected his family to the most influential lineages in colonial Virginia.

* '''Marriage:''' He was married to '''Winney King''', the granddaughter of '''John King of Norfolk'''.
* '''Children:'''
    * '''Joseph Francis Lewis''' (Lewis-9841), a master brickmason who built the first brick house in Kentucky, '''"Sportsman's Hill"'''. 
    * '''Sarah Lewis''' married '''Milner Redford''' in 1766. Joseph served as her father of record on the marriage bond, with his son '''John Lewis''' as security. Through Sarah, Joseph was the great-grandfather of '''Lewis Robards''', the first husband of '''Rachel Donelson''' (later the wife of President '''Andrew Jackson''').
    * '''Col. John Lewis''' married '''Mildred Lewis''', daughter of '''Col. Robert Lewis''' and '''Jane Meriwether'''. This strategic marriage linked Joseph's descendants directly to the '''Warner Hall line''', making his children distant cousins to '''George Washington''' through the Warner family ancestors.

=== Land and Legacy ===
Joseph Lewis was a man of substantial means, possessing the property and enslaved labor required of the "Planter" class. Research notes identify a will for a Joseph Lewis of Goochland that bequeathed enslaved people to heirs in Kentucky, reflecting the family's pivot toward the frontier in the late 1700s.

His status in the Virginia Tidewater and Piedmont regions provided the "genealogical capital" that enabled his children to transition successfully to the Kentucky frontier. Although Joseph remained a pillar of the Virginia Tidewater, his sons Joseph Francis and John, and his daughter Sarah, became instrumental in the settlement of the Kentucky wilderness. The elder Joseph Lewis represented the administrative power of the established Virginia colonial hierarchy, providing the social status that allowed his children to become leaders of the American expansion into the West.

== Research Notes ==

=== Identity and Origins of Joseph Francis Lewis (Lewis-9841) ===
* '''Traditional Narrative:''' Older accounts, such as ''Pioneer Families of Missouri'', mistakenly identify Joseph Francis Lewis as an immigrant from England. 
* '''Revised Narrative:''' Microfilm records from the ''Annals of Henrico Parish'' (A.G.L.L., F3652) identify him as a '''native Virginian''' from the Goochland/Henrico area.
* '''Vocation:''' He was a "skilled brickmason" who brought permanent architecture to the Kentucky frontier. His most famous work is '''"Sportsman's Hill,"''' the first brick house west of the Allegheny Mountains, built for his brother-in-law, Col. William Whitley.
* '''Spouse:''' He married '''Sarah Whitley''' (c. 1752–1837), sister of famed Kentucky pioneer and Indian fighter '''William Chapman Whitley'''.

=== Parentage: Joseph Lewis of Goochland and Winney King ===
* '''Father (Joseph Lewis):''' He was a prominent "Gentleman Vestryman" and '''Church Warden''' for Henrico Parish (St. John's Church) between 1752 and 1773. He also served as the '''Parish Collector''' and '''Sheriff of Henrico''' in 1766. 
* '''Mother (Winney King):''' Identified as the wife of Joseph Lewis and grandmother of Ruth Whitley Lewis. Her estate ties in Kentucky suggest she was the granddaughter of '''John King of Norfolk'''.
* '''Status:''' The family was part of the "Western Aristocracy," holding significant "genealogical capital" and social standing in the Virginia Tidewater and Piedmont before migrating west.

=== Strategic Kinship Ties ===
* '''The Washington Connection:''' Joseph Francis's brother, '''Col. John Lewis''', married '''Mildred Lewis'''. Mildred was the granddaughter of '''John Lewis and Elizabeth Warner'''. Elizabeth Warner's sister, '''Mildred Warner''', was the grandmother of '''George Washington'''.
* '''The Andrew Jackson Connection:''' Joseph Francis's sister, '''Sarah Lewis''', married '''Milner Redford''' (1738–1805). Their grandson, '''Lewis Robards''', was the first husband of '''Rachel Donelson''', who later married '''President Andrew Jackson'''.
* '''The Whitley-Hargrove Alliance:''' This branch converged on the frontier when Joseph Francis's daughter, '''Ruth Whitley Lewis''', married '''Valentine Hargrove''' in Lincoln County, Kentucky, on October 9, 1799.

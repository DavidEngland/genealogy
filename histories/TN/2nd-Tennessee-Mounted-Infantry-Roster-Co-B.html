Additional Information from NARA Records

The following information is taken from the compiled service records of the 2nd Tennessee Mounted Infantry, NARA Microfilm Publication No. 395, rolls number 136, 137, and 138. Additional information provided only on those men who died or were listed as deserted on the Company Roster.

Name on AG's report – Name on NARA microfilm – additional information:

MARTIN, Samuel H.

MARTIN, Samuel H. – muster rolls show promotion as Captain 13 Nov 1864 dated 27 Sep 64. 10 Aug 1864 died of wounds and disease at Clifton.

BECKHAM, William H.

BECKHAM, William H. – Captured in Hardin Co. TN 27 May 1864. Exchanged at Savannah, 25 Nov 1864. Went to Annapolis where he received two months pay (all he ever received). Says he drew at Annapolis one overcoat, one jacket, one pr pants, two shirts, two pr drawers, 2 pr socks. POW record: Captured at Clifton, TN 27 May 1864 – no record of confinement at Andersonville, GA. Paroled at Savannah GA 26 Nov 1864 reported to Camp Parole MD 3 Dec 1864. Muster roll says captured near Indian Creek.

FRANKS, William

FRANKS, William – reported captured on Indian Creek 27 May 1864 – reported captured near Clifton 28 May 64. 29 Aug 64 reported died at Andersonville, Grave No. 7244 of debilitas.

HAINES, Allen

HAYNES, Allen – Recruit on detached service Hardin Co., TN and cut off from command 1 Sep 1864 – described as born in Wayne Co., TN, age 18, blue eyes, light hair, fair complexion, 5'8″.

JOHNSON, Isaac

JOHNSON, Isaac – POW record: captured Hardin Co., TN, paroled at Savannah, GA on 26 Nov 1864, reported to CGB, MD 1 Dec 1864, sent to Camp Parole MD 2 Dec 1864, reported at Camp Parole 3 Dec 1864. Furloughed 11 Dec 1864 for 30 days to report to Camp Chase, OH, Deserted from furlough, failed to report 15 Feb 1865.

KELLY, John W.

KELLY, John W. – reported 1 Sep 1864 on detached service Hardin Co., TN, cut off from command. War Department Notation 4 Dec 1889 – mustered out to date 17 Oct 1864.

STRICKLIN, Samuel H.

Not carried on NARA records.

LENVILLE, John

LENVILLE, John – 5 June 1864 reported died of disease on Cypress Creek, Wayne Co., TN.

MAYBERRY, James

MABERRY, James – 5 June 1864 reported died of disease at Clifton, TN. War Department Notation 12 Feb 1890: died of erysipelas 15 June 1864 in Reg. Hospital.

RISNER, John

RISNER, John – reported 13 March 1864, died at Clifton TN of disease.

WESSON, William A.

WESSON, William A. – reported 17 Dec 1864, died at Waynesboro, TN of disease.

PRINCE, John L.

PRINCE, John L. – reported 30 April 1864 deserted. War Department Notation, 9 Oct 1885, application for removal of charge of desertion denied.

CLARK, Thomas M.

CLARK, Thomas M. – reported deserted at Clifton 21 Aug 1864 and charged for one horse at $120.00.

MELTON, William

MELTON, William – reported 10 Aug 1864, deserted at Clifton, TN.

NORTHCUT, James L.

NORTHCUTT, James – reported 10 Aug 1864 deserted at Clifton. War Department Notation 22 July 1887, application for honorable discharge denied.

WILBANKS, Richard

WILLBANKS, Richard – reported 25 March 1864 deserted from Clifton, TN.

BOYD, Wesley

BOYD, Wesley – 6 Feb 1864 ordered arrested as deserted from 6th Tennessee Cavalry, turned over to that command by Maj. MURPHY.

CARTER, William

CARTER, William – reported 30 April 1864 as present.

Research Notes

Military Service Context

Company B of the 2nd Tennessee Mounted Infantry was organized in October 1863, during a period when Union forces were working to secure East Tennessee and parts of Middle Tennessee. Most soldiers enlisted on October 15, 1863, at what was likely a mass enlistment event.

Geographic Service Area

The company operated primarily in:

  • Wayne County, Tennessee – Home county for many soldiers
  • Hardin County, Tennessee – Location of Clifton, headquarters and major engagement area
  • Tennessee River region – Strategic military importance

Casualties and Desertions

Company B experienced:

  • Deaths in service: At least 5 soldiers died of disease or wounds
  • POW deaths: William Franks died at Andersonville Prison
  • Desertions: 5+ soldiers deserted, some applications for removal of desertion charges were denied post-war
  • Killed by irregulars: Branson Davis was killed by bushwackers while on detached service

Family Connections

Wayne County families were tightly interconnected through marriage and kinship. The Horton, Brewer, Lawson, Atkinson, and other families represented in this roster frequently intermarried, creating extensive family networks that influenced military service patterns.

== Biography ==

'''Susannah Lewis''' (1717–1792) was born in Middlesex County, Virginia, and became the wife of '''Valentine Ball''' (1706–1770). Her life spanned the transition from Tidewater planter society to the Virginia frontier, and her ancestry remains a subject of active genealogical debate.

=== Vital Records ===
The following vitals are consistently supported by parish registers and family studies:
* '''Birth:''' April 23, 1717, in Middlesex County, Virginia
* '''Christening:''' May 26, 1717, in Christ Church Parish, Middlesex County
* '''Marriage:''' January 31, 1733, to Valentine Ball in Christ Church Parish (she was approximately 15 or 16 years old)
* '''Death:''' Circa July 1792 in Amherst County, Virginia

=== Geographic Context and Migration ===
Susannah remained in the '''Ball's Neck/Burnham Creek''' neighborhood of Middlesex County for several decades, raising a large family. By the 1760s, she and Valentine migrated westward to '''Amherst County''', transitioning from Tidewater planters to colonial frontiersmen. Her presence there is confirmed by her role as '''executrix of her husband's 1769 will''', in which she was granted life interest in the plantation.

Note: Some records list a death date of January 12, 1769, but this is actually the date her husband composed his will naming her as executrix, not her death date.

=== Disputed Parentage and Ancestry ===
Three distinct theories exist regarding Susannah's parents, making this a common point of debate for this profile:

'''The Tidewater/Middlesex Theory (Traditional):'''
* Many records identify her as the daughter of '''John Lewis''' and '''Elizabeth''' of Middlesex
* ''The American Genealogist'' (TAG) research specifically links her to the immigrant '''Thomas O'Brissell''' and '''Henry Nichols''', proposing that her grandparents were '''John Lewis''' (a carpenter) and '''Elizabeth O'Brissell'''
* '''WikiTree Conflict:''' She has been detached from John Lewis and Elizabeth O'Brissell as parents, with the claim that John died two years before her birth and Elizabeth was too old to be her mother

'''The Spotsylvania Theory:'''
* Some records attempt to link her to '''John Zachary Lewis and Mary Walker''' of Spotsylvania
* However, the Zachary Lewis family records show a daughter Susannah Lewis (b. c. 1708) who married '''Joseph Collins Jr.''' in 1728, suggesting this is a different individual entirely

'''The "Western Aristocracy" Tradition:'''
* Family narratives associate her with the broader, influential Lewis network of Virginia, characterizing her as a woman of "quality" and "stewardship" from a family of "law books" and steady judgment

=== Family Connections ===
Research in the Ball's Neck neighborhood tracks land titles that link Susannah's lineage to the following families:
* '''O'Brissell and Nichols:''' Her proposed ancestors who settled neighboring tracts in the 1650s
* '''Whitley-Hargrove Alliance:''' Through her daughter '''Mary Ball''' (who married '''John Washington Hargrove'''), Susannah is the grandmother of '''Valentine Hargrove''', who married '''Ruth Whitley Lewis''' in 1799, merging the Ball-Lewis line with the famed Kentucky pioneers

'''Comment 1 - Vital Records Documentation:'''

* Christening: May 26, 1717, in Christ Church Parish, Middlesex County
* Marriage: January 31, 1733, to Valentine Ball in Christ Church Parish

'''Comment 2 - Geographic Migration:'''

Susannah lived in the Ball's Neck/Burnham Creek neighborhood of Middlesex County for several decades before migrating to Amherst County in the 1760s. She served as executrix of her husband's 1769 will and was granted life interest in the plantation. This demonstrates her role as a woman of property and stewardship during the transition from Tidewater society to the Virginia frontier.

'''Comment 3 - Parentage Theories:'''

There are three competing theories regarding Susannah's parentage:

1. '''Tidewater/Middlesex Theory:''' Links her to John Lewis (carpenter) and Elizabeth O'Brissell, with connections to immigrant Thomas O'Brissell and Henry Nichols. This is supported by The American Genealogist (TAG) research and land title patterns in the Ball's Neck neighborhood.

2. '''Spotsylvania Theory:''' Attempts to link her to John Zachary Lewis and Mary Walker, but this appears to confuse her with a different Susannah Lewis (b. c. 1708) who married Joseph Collins Jr. in 1728.

3. '''Western Aristocracy Tradition:''' Family narratives connecting her to the broader influential Lewis network of Virginia.

The current detachment from John Lewis and Elizabeth O'Brissell was based on chronological concerns. Further research using Ruth and Sam Sparacio's publications (Antient Press) on Ball's Neck land titles may help resolve this dispute.

'''Comment 4 - Family Connections:'''

Through her daughter Mary Ball (who married John Washington Hargrove), Susannah is the grandmother of Valentine Hargrove, who married Ruth Whitley Lewis in 1799. This connection merged the Ball-Lewis line with the famed Kentucky pioneers, including the Whitley family.

== Biography ==

**Alexander Sells McDonald (1738–1793)** stands as the patriarch of a significant branch of the McDonald lineage, representing the migration of **Clan Donald** from Scotland to the American frontier.

=== Early Life and Origins ===

Alexander was born circa **1738 in Wigtown, Wigtownshire, Scotland**, a region in the southwest of Scotland. He carried the proud heritage of **Clan Donald**, one of Scotland's most powerful Highland clans, whose ancient maxim proclaimed: **"It is no joy without Clan Donald"**. This heritage traced back through centuries to **Somerled** (d. 1164), the great Norse-Gaelic warlord, and **Donald of the Isles** (d. 1289), characterized as a lineage of "warrior herdsmen."

=== Marriage and Family ===

Alexander married **Hannah Bell** (1736–1835), who was born in the Colony of Virginia. Hannah was a remarkable woman who lived to the extraordinary age of 99, eventually dying in **Overton, Tennessee**. Their marriage produced a family that would become foundational to the Tennessee frontier.

=== Migration to the American Frontier ===

The McDonald family migrated from Virginia into the frontier regions of Tennessee, participating in the great westward expansion that shaped the American South. Alexander died on **May 21, 1793**, in **Washington, Virginia**, but his descendants continued the journey westward.

=== Children ===

Alexander and Hannah's children included:

* **Reuben McDonald** (c. 1766–1830) – Born in Virginia, he died in **Knoxville, Tennessee**.
* **Allen McDonald** (c. 1773–1842) – Born in Virginia, he became a foundational figure in **Overton, Tennessee**, where he died in 1842. Allen married **Susannah Sells** (1788–1855), daughter of the pioneer **Solomon Sells**. This marriage solidified the connection to the Sells family, which is reflected in Alexander's own recorded middle name in historical sources.

=== Legacy and Cultural Heritage ===

==== Clan Donald Identity ====

The McDonald family maintained their deep identification with **Clan Donald**, preserving their Scottish heritage even as they adapted to frontier life in America. The ancient clan traditions and pride in their warrior ancestry remained central to family identity.

==== Technical Craftsmanship ====

The McDonald family became renowned for their technical craftsmanship. Many of Alexander's descendants, particularly those who settled in **Lauderdale County, Alabama**, achieved fame as skilled **blacksmiths and gunsmiths**. They were particularly noted for producing precision **"Kentucky Rifles"**, the long rifles that became essential to frontier life and warfare.

==== Military and Civic Leadership ====

The family produced leaders in various fields, including:

* **Captain Franklin Pierce Scott** – A descendant who served in the Civil War
* **Stephen McDonald** – Served in the **Alabama Legislature**

==== Name Variations ====

In genealogical research, the family name often appears interchangeably as **McDonald, MacDonald, or McDaniel**, reflecting common spelling variations of Scottish surnames in American records.

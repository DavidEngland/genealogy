== Biography ==

William Wesley Hargrove was born on 4 January 1816 in Alabama, United States. He was the son of [[Hargrove-277|Valentine Hargrove]] and [[Lewis-8883|Ruth Whitley Lewis]], who were 39 and 36 years old at the time of his birth.

He married Dicey Caruthers in 1837 in Tennessee. They became the parents of at least ten children—five sons and five daughters—born across Tennessee and Mississippi during the years of westward migration and early settlement.

For more than a decade, William lived in Tippah County, Mississippi, where he appears in the 1840, 1850, and 1860 federal censuses. By 1870, the family had moved north into the Ozarks, settling in Mount Vernon Township, Lawrence County, Missouri.

On 26 October 1871, after more than thirty years of marriage, William married Paulena E. Alexander in Lawrence County, Missouri.

During the Civil War, William served in the Union Army as a Captain in Company C, 14th Regiment, Missouri State Militia Cavalry, a unit organized for internal defense and counter-guerrilla operations in Missouri.

In the final years of his life, William migrated once more—this time to the Pacific Northwest. He died in 1876 in Marion County, Oregon, at about 60 years of age.

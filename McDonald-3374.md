== Biography ==

'''Thomas Anderson McDonald''' was born circa 1822 in Wartrace, Bedford County, Tennessee.<ref name="birth">Family records compiled by William Lindsey McDonald and Lois Marveline England Henderson. Note: One source lists birth date as August 28, 1821, in Pennsylvania, which conflicts with family records and census data supporting Tennessee birth.</ref> He was the son of [[McDonald-6638|John McDonald]] and [[Emory-131|Margaret Emory]]. He married Sarah A. Adkisson on June 24, 1852, in Lawrence County, Tennessee.<ref name="marriage">"Tennessee, County Marriages, 1790-1950", FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:Q2ZS-4W7V), Entry for Thomas A McDonald and Sarah A Adkisson, 24 June 1852, Lawrence, Tennessee</ref> He died circa 1885 in Savannah, Hardin County, Tennessee.<ref name="death">Family narratives state death circa 1885; however, another record in family sources places death around October 28, 1900. Further research needed to reconcile this discrepancy.</ref> He was buried in Savannah Cemetery, Savannah, Hardin County, Tennessee.

=== Early Life ===

Thomas Anderson was the fourth child of John McDonald and Margaret Emory. He was born in Wartrace, Bedford County, Tennessee, during a period of rapid settlement following his family's migration from South Carolina. The McDonald family was part of the great southern migration westward in the early 19th century and maintained strong ties to their Scots-Celtic heritage and the traditions of Clan Donald.

=== Marriage and Family ===

Thomas married Sarah A. Adkisson (also recorded as Atkinson) on June 24, 1852, in Lawrence County, Tennessee.<ref name="marriage" /> Sarah was raised on her father's homestead at Wayland Springs in Lawrence County. The couple resided at Wayland Springs for several years following their marriage.

=== Occupation ===

Thomas Anderson McDonald was a skilled blacksmith, continuing the family tradition of technical craftsmanship.<ref name="occupation">Family records compiled by William Lindsey McDonald and Lois Marveline England Henderson</ref> The McDonald family was noted for producing "Kentucky Rifles" and high-quality metalwork, with Thomas's father and brothers being renowned for their craftsmanship.

=== Residences ===

Thomas and his family lived in several locations throughout the Tennessee-Alabama borderlands:<ref name="residences">Census records and family documentation compiled by William Lindsey McDonald and Lois Marveline England Henderson</ref>

* '''Bedford County, Tennessee''' – Place of birth (1822)
* '''Wayland Springs, Lawrence County, Tennessee''' – Where he married Sarah and lived for several years; daughter Palina was born here in 1859
* '''Wayne County, Tennessee''' – Listed in the 1860 Census<ref name="census1860">"United States Census, 1860", Wayne County, Tennessee</ref>
* '''Cypress Creek (near Parsonage Chapel), Lauderdale County, Alabama''' – Resided here by 1870; neighbor to his brother [[McDonald-2|John Scruggs McDonald]]<ref name="census1870">"United States Census, 1870", FamilySearch (https://familysearch.org/ark:/61903/1:1:MHK8-BC5), Entry for Anderson McDonald, 1870, Lauderdale, Alabama</ref>
* '''Cloverdale/Rawhide, Lauderdale County, Alabama''' – Resided here in 1881 during the Frank and Jesse James gang robbery at Lock Six on the Muscle Shoals Canal

=== Death and Burial ===

Thomas Anderson McDonald died in Savannah, Hardin County, Tennessee. Family narratives indicate a death date of circa 1885, though another record in family sources places his death around October 28, 1900.<ref name="death" /> He was buried in Savannah Cemetery, Savannah, Hardin County, Tennessee. His son Commodore Harry McDonald later lived with his brother Frank in Savannah and was also buried there.<ref name="burial">Family records compiled by William Lindsey McDonald and Lois Marveline England Henderson</ref>

=== Research Notes ===

'''Surname Variations:''' Researchers should note that the McDonald family name was frequently recorded as "McDaniel" in census reports and other official documents. This was a common variation for the McDonald/MacDonald family during this period.<ref name="surname">Census records 1850-1880, various counties</ref>

'''Birth Date Discrepancy:''' One source lists Thomas's birth as August 28, 1821, in Pennsylvania, which likely needs correction to the Tennessee location (circa 1822 in Wartrace, Bedford County) supported by family records and census data.<ref name="birth" />

'''Death Date Discrepancy:''' Family sources contain conflicting information regarding the date of death (circa 1885 versus October 28, 1900). Further research is needed to resolve this discrepancy.<ref name="death" />

W. B. Parrish Gord [Guardian] settlement
State of Tennessee. To the worshipful County Court of Wayne County. M. W. Jones, Clerk of said Court, and William B. Parrish, Guardian for James, John & Mary Jane Parish, minor heirs of Isaac Parish, Decd, would submit for your consideration the following settlement to wit:
Guardian — Dr.
* To amt. Recd from Richard Olive: $30.00
* To amt. Recd from Jas M Reynolds: $60.00
* To amt. Recd from Sarah Olive: $36.10
* To amt. Recd from [unclear] Gill: $12.60
* Interest on the above from 1853 to date: $27.46
* Total Assets: $166.16 [Note: OCR states $153.16, but calculation adjusted per standard interest and carry-over.]
Guardian — Cr. [Credits]
* By Amt paid James A. Parrish per receipt: $47.55
* By amt paid for Bond: $2.00
* By amt paid Tax Bill in Waynesboro: $1.00
* Allowance to Guardian for 1855, 6, 7, 8 & to date: $5.00
* Amt. paid Clerk for settlement: $1.00
* Recording same: $0.50
* Total Credits: $58.05
Bal Due John & Mary Jane to date: $95.11
March 7 1859 Wm Jones Clerk W. B. Parrish

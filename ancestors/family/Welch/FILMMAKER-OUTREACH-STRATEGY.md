# Filmmaker Outreach Strategy: "Tory Diaspora"

## Pitching the Nicholas Welch Story to Directors & Producers

## THE BIGGER PICTURE: Land Grants, Speculation and Fraud — How It Shaped America

Nicholas Welch's story is ultimately about **land**: how Americans acquired it, lost it, speculated on it, and sometimes defrauded each other for it. His journey from 600-acre Pennsylvania farmer to exiled Loyalist to Charleston-based land speculator with 6,000 acres illuminates the shadowy world of early American land markets—the Yazoo land fraud era, the refugee economy of buying worthless titles, Spanish grants in contested territories, legitimate grants mixed with questionable dealings. **For Ron Howard and other filmmakers: Nicholas Welch is the profile of a land speculator in the era when America's territorial ambitions were built on paper claims, surveying skills, and cutting corners.** This is the untold story of how land speculation and fraud shaped the American frontier.

### **META-DOCUMENTARY APPROACH** (Burns or Howard)

**The Format:** Documentary as Journey of Discovery

**Concept:** Rather than just TELLING Nicholas Welch's story, document the process of FINDING it. Travel to the archives, show the documents being discovered in real time, build suspense around whether certain documents even exist.

**Why This Works:**
- **Suspense:** "Will we find Cornwallis's signature? Let's look..."
- **Accessibility:** Audience travels with filmmaker to National Archives (Kew, UK), South Carolina archives, Tennessee courthouses
- **Educational:** Shows HOW historical research actually works
- **Meta-narrative:** The search for the documents parallels Nicholas's own journey

**Structure (2-hour documentary or 3-part series):**

**Act 1: The Hunt Begins**
- Modern-day introduction: Why this story matters
- First archive visit: National Archives, Kew, UK

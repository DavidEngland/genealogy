#### Post #2: "99 Years Old: Sarah Welch's Incredible Life"

"In 1850, census takers in Giles County, Tennessee encountered a remarkable woman: Sarah Welch, age 99.

Born around 1751 in Virginia, Sarah witnessed:
• The French & Indian War
• The American Revolution (married a Loyalist Major during it)
• Formation of the United States
• War of 1812
• Mexican-American War
• California Gold Rush
• Invention of railroad, telegraph, photography
• And was still alive as the nation hurtled toward Civil War

She married Nicholas Welch in Philadelphia on December 26, 1794, when she was 43 and he was 54 - both past their youth, both probably seeking companionship rather than romance.

She followed him to Louisiana Territory, then to Tennessee. When he died in December 1814, she was 63 - and she lived another 37 YEARS.

Imagine the stories she could tell. The changes she witnessed. The memories of 'Major Nicholas' and his incredible journeys.

By 1851, when Sarah died at approximately 100 years old, nearly everyone who remembered the Revolutionary War firsthand was gone. She was one of the last.

And now, we barely remember Sarah.

This post is for Sarah Welch and all the women who lived extraordinary lives but left few records behind.

Share if you have a long-lived ancestor whose stories deserve to be told.

#WomensHistory #Longevity #1850s #RevolutionaryWar #Widows #Tennessee #Genealogy #FamilyHistory"

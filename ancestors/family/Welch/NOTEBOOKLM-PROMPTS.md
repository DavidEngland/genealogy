## SECTION 1: AUDIO OVERVIEW GENERATION

### Podcast-Style Audio Summary

**Prompt for NotebookLM Audio Overview:**

```
Create a podcast-style audio discussion between two hosts about Major Nicholas 
Welch (c.1740-1814), a Loyalist officer in the American Revolution who was 
exiled, banished, lost everything, petitioned the King in London for 
compensation, and then remarkably returned to America to rebuild his life.

Focus on:
1. The dramatic irony of his choices (fought for losing side)
2. His incredible journey across 8 locations: Pennsylvania → North Carolina → 
   Georgia → East Florida → Bahamas → London → South Carolina → Louisiana → Tennessee
3. The Battle of Ramsour's Mill (June 20, 1780) where he lost three sons as prisoners
4. His wife Elizabeth dying during the exile period (c.1780-1782)
5. His petition to the Crown yielding only £448 (39% of his £1,150 claim)
6. His courageous/foolish decision to return to the United States (the country 
   that banished him)
7. His reinvention as a land speculator (6,000 acres in SC by 1793)
8. His final years as a Tennessee pioneer

Emphasize the human drama, the family tragedy, and the American themes of 
resilience, reconciliation, and reinvention. This is a story about choosing 
the "wrong" side and what happened next.

Make it engaging for a general audience interested in American history, not 
just genealogists.
```

**Expected Output:** 8-12 minute podcast-style audio conversation

Tory Diaspora — The Nicholas Welch Story

Core Logline (Refined for Pitch Rooms)

After losing 600 acres fighting for the British Crown, Loyalist Major Nicholas Welch is exiled to the Bahamas, petitions London with Cornwallis testifying on his behalf, receives only £448 in compensation—and returns to America to transform that sum into 6,000 acres through surveying skill, refugee land markets, Spanish grants, and Yazoo-era speculation. His life exposes the paper empire beneath the American frontier.

This is a Revolution story from the losing side—about land, reinvention, and moral compromise.

Nicholas Welch of NC, SC, TN, GA, AL (revised)by Larry Petrisky

The early census and land records reveal six men of this name born before 1770, and four more born before 1799 in North Carolina, South Carolina, Tennessee, Georgia and Alabama. Thanks to Marcia McClure, Mike Welch, Jerry Butler, Pat Welch, Mary Winham, BennieLou Altom, Dorothy Butler Welsh, Brian (Gene) Welch and others for their insights and research which I appreciate because the Welches are not my line and they are quite difficult. My tentative analysis of these men is as follows, keeping in mind that conflicts still exist and erroneous conclusions are my fault:


(1) Nicholas Welch Sr. (b.1740 Lancaster Co, PA d.1814 Giles Co, TN)
Son of John Welch (b.1716 d.1769 Tryon Co, NC) and Elizabeth Whitesides, Welsh or German Lutherans of Lancaster Co, Pennsylvania. Grandson of William Welsh d.c.1748.
m(1) Elizabeth (Moore?—dau of Moses Moore of Lancaster Co, PA then Tryon Co, NC)
m(2) Sarah b.1751 VA d. aft 1850 Giles Co, TN

1737 - John Welsh granted 150 acres in Lancaster County, PA, on land next to Rev. John
Casper Stoever (b. 1707 emig. 1728 to PA, d. 1779 VA).
6 Feb 1738 -John Welsh m. Elizabeth Whitesides by Rev. John Casper Stoever in Lancaster Co, PA (she d.bef. 1742). These two events support a birthdate of 1716 for John Welch and 1740 for Nicholas Welch, and verify birthplace of Nicholas.
15 Dec 1739 -Jacob Wolfe Welsch m. Elizabeth -----by Rev. John Casper Stoever in Lancaster Co, PA. (Jacob’s first son was named John Jacob Welsh.Jacob Welsh had a brother William Welsh who’s first son was named John Peter Welsh, both Johns were baptized by Rev. Stoever in 1741. Our John Welch also had a brother William so it is possible that Jacob and John and William were brothers. These other Johns had sons or grandsons named Nicholas.)
10 May 1748 Moses Moor m(2) ------- Gillighin by Rev. John Casper Stoever in Lancaster Co, PA. (Father of Lt. Col John Moore of Lincoln Co, NC. John Moore d.1789Bertie Co, NC. The Moores were Baptists, soldiers, gunsmiths/blacksmiths in NC.)

24 Oct 1759 – John Welch granted land on waters of Catawba River in Anson Co, NC (in what became Mecklenburg then Tryon then Lincoln Counties). In 1761 John Welsh filed a survey for this land, Nicholas Welsh was one of the chainbearers.

11 Feb 1762 William Welch (b.c.1734 VA d.1818 SC),cousin of Nicholas Welch Sr, is granted 150a on Lynches Creek (River) in Craven Co (later Cheraws District), SC. (Lynches R. divided Camden & Cheraws Districts.) William remained on this land until 1797, according to his estate records filed 1818 Greenville Co, SC.

12 July 1766 William Welch, of Craven Co, SC, sold to Nicholas Welch ofNC, 170a on Howards or Haywards Creek, Mecklenburg Co, NC. (23 Jan 1767 Thomas Welch,
brother of Nicholas Welch Sr, granted 300 a on Howard or Hayward Creek in Mecklenburg Co, NC.)(Tryon County formed from Mecklenburg County in 1769. The North Carolina Gazateer by William S. Powell says the following about Indian Creek:
Indian Creek rises in sw Catawba County flows se across Lincoln County, dips briefly into nw Gaston County and flows ne back into Lincoln County where it enters South Fork Catawba River. Appears on the Collet map, 1770 as Howards Creek, but as Indian Creek on the Price map, 1808. See Betty Kuykendall Price’s post # 22927 on the Moore Forum at www.genealogy.genforum.comfor location of Moses Moore and his son John Moore.)

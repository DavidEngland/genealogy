Ex James Welsh

In the Name of God Amen Deceased ⎬ I James Willsh of the Township of Paxton & Coun ⌨ ┃ ty of Lancaster and provance of pensilvenia Biing Very Sick and Weak of body but of perfite mind and memory and Calling to mind the mortality of the body that it is appointed for all men Conce 57 once to Die and there for I Recommend my Soul to God that give it and my Body to the Earth to be Bured in a Cristan and Destant maner and as for worldly Goods that God heath been plised to Bestow on me I Desposs of in the maner and form as foloweth vizfirst I give and bequeath to my beloved wife Mary Welsh all the housell furneter with the one third part of the Cows and Sheep with one horse and her mentinence out of the pleace During her Widowhood or till the two youngest Boys . viz . Robert and Joseph Coms to Eage and in Cease that Shee Sees fill to alter her pleas of Reasedance shee is to have one hundred powns from my four youngest Sons Viz . Thomas James and Joseph - Secondly I give and bequeath unto my son John forty pounds to be payed in the year of our Lord 1700 - Thirdly I give and bequeath to my son in Law William Peterson ten pounds to be payed in the year 1759 - forthly I give and bequeath unto my son Thomas one Hundred and fifty acres of Land from the midell of the Great Swamp and Backward to the East Corner and one horse named Buck - fifthly I give and Bequeath unto my son James one hundred Excors of Land from the midell of the Great Swamp & Backwords Joyning James McKnaught Sixthly I give and Bequeath to my two Sons Robert and Joseph the Remainder of the and next the River . Seventhly I give and bequeath to my Daughter Jean one Bay horse with a larg Blise in his forehead and asid Seadell and Bridell with forty pounds of movey to be payed in the year 17 . 59 Eightly I give and bequeath to my Daughter Ezabel fiftey pounds when come to Edge - Ninthly I give and bequeath to my Daughter Mary fifty pounds when shee Come to age - N : B that these foure sons Thomas James and Joseph is to pay all Debts and Legfies at cording as Each of them posesses of the Land and I order and appoint my will beloved wife Marey Wallsh and son John - Wellsh my Executors Revoking all other Wills and Exr what so ever as wittness my hand and Seal this 21 Day of January 1754 I order the Sarvant Man Michel McDaniel for the Benifet of the plantation and Thomas to be his Master and I order that the two youngest have ther Vettls and Cloths untill they befit to Doe for them Selfs - James J Wellsh : L . S . - Wittness Richard McClure - Wm. Anderson - The 3th day May 1754 Before me the Subscriber came Richard McClure and William Anderson the Witnesses to the within written will and on their Corporal ⌨ did declare and said that they were present and saw and heard James Welch the Testator Sign Seal Publish & declare the within writing as and for his last Will and 1 Testament )
Testament & that at the doing thereof he was of Sound and disposing mind memory & understanding according to the best of their Knowledge Observation & Belief . Edwd Shippen D. Mr

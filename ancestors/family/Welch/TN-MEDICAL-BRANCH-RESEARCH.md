# The Tennessee Medical Branch: Nicholas Welch (#5) Descendants

## Wayne County, Lawrence County, and Hardin County Medical Connections

---

## OVERVIEW

**Nicholas Welch (#5)** (c.1764 Craven Co, SC - 1822 Wayne Co, TN) and his descendants established a branch of the Welch family in **Wayne County, Tennessee** that produced physicians and connected to prominent medical families through marriage. User research notes mention "doctors" or "colorful ladies that were wives of doctors" in **Lawrence, Wayne, and Hardin County, Tennessee**.

This document serves as a research guide to document these medical profession connections and identify the specific individuals involved.

---

## NICHOLAS WELCH #5: THE FOUNDER

### Vital Information

**Born:** c.1764, Craven County, South Carolina  
**Died:** 1822, Wayne County, Tennessee  
**Father:** Benjamin Welch (b.1742 VA, d.c.1802 GA)  
**Grandfather:** Thomas Welch (d.1752 Fauquier Co, VA)  
**Great-grandfather:** Cousin of John Welsh (d.1769 Tryon Co, NC)

**Marriage:** Margaret Whitmore of Laurens District, SC  
*Daughter of Joseph Whitmore (1720-1804) of Duncans Creek, Laurens County, SC*

### Geographic Journey

1. **Virginia Origin** - Prince William/Fauquier Counties (1752-1763)
2. **French-Indian War Service** - Virginia Regiment (1760)
3. **South Carolina Settlement** - Lynches Creek area (1763-1787)
4. **Craven/Laurens District** - Birth of Nicholas, marriage to Margaret (1764-1802)
5. **Georgia Ventures** - Greene County, trading (1787-1794)
6. **Return to South Carolina** - Pendleton/Laurens Districts (1790-1811)
7. **Tennessee Migration** - Wayne County (by 1820-1822)

### Key Family Connections

**Whitmore Family** (his wife's family):
- Joseph Whitmore (1720-1804) - father-in-law
- George Whitmore - brother-in-law (estate disputed 1811)
- Elizabeth (Whitmore) Watson - sister-in-law, married John Watson Jr.

**Associated Families in Laurens District, SC (1790):**
- George Whitmore family
- John Watson family
- Solomon Duty (Douty) family
- Moses Duncan family
- David Jones family

- Huddleston family

*All these families connected by marriage or land transactions*
